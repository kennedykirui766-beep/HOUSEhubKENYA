About HomeHub

Simplifying property management for landlords, tenants, and service providers across Kenya.

Who We Are

HomeHub is a comprehensive property management platform designed to streamline the rental experience for everyone involved.

Our Mission

"To make rental and property management seamless, transparent, and efficient through technology."

Our Story

Property management in Kenya has long been plagued by challenges: late rent payments, communication gaps between landlords and tenants, inefficient maintenance processes, and poor record-keeping.

Recognizing these pain points, our team of real estate professionals and technology experts came together to create HomeHub - a platform that addresses these challenges head-on.

How We Solve It

  • Automated Rent Collection:
    Timely payments integrated with M-Pesa.
  • Centralized Communication:
    Direct messaging between landlords, tenants, and providers.
  • Efficient Maintenance:
    Quick connection to verified service providers.
  • Digital Record-Keeping:
    Secure storage of leases, payments, and documents.

What We Do

Property Management

Service Connection

Analytics

Real-time analytics and financial reporting for informed decision-making.
